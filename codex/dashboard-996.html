Clear engine fault codes?

This erases stored emissions codes and freeze-frame data, and resets emissions readiness monitors. It does not repair the underlying fault.

Park the car, turn the engine off, and leave the ignition on. The backend checks engine speed and road speed before clearing.

Standard engine emissions codes only. Other modules and manufacturer-specific faults may remain.

pdiag is open source and read-only. It never writes to the car.
